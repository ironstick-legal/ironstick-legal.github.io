# Manual - Quick Start

> IRONSTICK quick start: install, licence, user and language, AI, signature, letterhead and client import.

Original page: https://www.ironstick.legal/manualquickstart.html

---

Quick start — from installation to your first clients in six steps.

## 1. Install IRONSTICK

![The IRONSTICK setup: language, destination folder, desktop shortcut, ready to install, installing](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/screens/A%20installer.png)

The setup in five short steps.

The [setup](https://www.ironstick.legal/download.html) installs IRONSTICK on your Windows computer in a few clicks.
You choose the language of the setup, the folder, and whether a desktop shortcut is created; then the [program](https://www.ironstick.legal/download.html) is installed.
If IRONSTICK is already installed, simply install the [new version](https://www.ironstick.legal/download.html) over it — your data stays where it is.

### What you do here

#### Setup

- **Setup language.** Choose the language in which the setup talks to you and confirm with *OK*.
- **Destination folder.** The suggested folder is *C:\Program Files\IRONSTICK*. Keep it, or choose another one with *Browse…*.
- **Desktop shortcut.** Leave the tick set if you want an IRONSTICK icon on the desktop.
- **Install.** The summary shows folder and shortcut once more; *Install* starts the installation, and a progress bar shows how far it is.

#### First start

- **Licence key.** On the first start IRONSTICK asks for your licence key. Type it in from the paper you received, or paste it.
- **Security code.** After a valid licence key you see a QR code. Scan it with an authenticator app on your phone. From then on, IRONSTICK opens only after you have entered the six-digit code from that app.
- **New phone.** If you lose your phone, enter the licence key again on the start screen; the QR code appears again and you set up the new phone.

## 2. Licence, user and language

![Configuration with the licence key dialog and the opened user card with the language field](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/screens/A%20config%20license%20user%20language.png)

The licence key, your user card and the language of the program.

Open the configuration with the gear in the top bar.
Here you enter or renew your licence key, fill in your user card and choose the language in which IRONSTICK and the AI talk to you.
Take a minute for the user card: your details are used in letters, pleadings and the licence request.

1. **Licence key.** The link *Licence key* under the version line opens the licence window. Enter or paste the key and click *Activate*. *Request license key* checks that your user card is complete and prepares the request text with subject and recipient, each with a copy button.
2. **User.** Opens your user card. The number next to the title identifies your installation; the small symbol copies it.
3. **Fields from the licence.** Company, salutation, first name and last name come from your licence key and are locked (padlock). Fill in the other fields: position, address, country, phone, mobile, e-mail, date of birth and the messengers you use.
4. **Language.** The language of the program and of the AI dialog. After *Save*, every screen switches to the new language at once.

## 3. AI configuration

![AI configuration with the free background services and the AI services for the built-in chat](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/screens/A%20config%20AI.png)

Free services for background tasks, and your AI services for the built-in chat.

The AI configuration holds the access keys (API keys) of the AI services IRONSTICK may use.
There are two kinds: free services that only do background tasks, and the services for the full AI functions of the built-in chat.
You need an account with the respective service; the links below each field lead to its sign-up, terms and privacy pages.

1. **AI configuration.** Opens the card with all AI services.
2. **Free services for background tasks.** Used only for background work such as e-mail assignment, the data monitor, the daily briefing and the situation report. Click the link below the field to create an account directly with the provider and generate an API key there. Paste this API key into the field and click *Save*. The toggle switch next to the service turns that service on or off for these activities — even while its API key stays stored. The red notice tells you what you agree to: with a free offer, data is passed to that provider — read its terms and privacy policy carefully with regard to the GDPR before you switch it on.
3. **AI services for the built-in chat.** Click the link below the field to create an account directly with the provider and generate an API key there. Paste the API key into the field and click *Save*. **Please note:** a working API key always costs money at the provider; a top-up of at least 20 dollars/euros per provider is recommended. We also recommend using at least two providers to avoid interruptions: if several services are stored, IRONSTICK uses them automatically in the fixed order shown by the numbers, the lowest-cost first; if one is unavailable or its credit is used up, the next one takes over. Check the balance of each account regularly.

## 4. Your signature

![The signature card with the drawing field and the buttons to clear, upload, delete and save](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/screens/A%20config%20sign.png)

Draw your signature once — or upload it as an image.

Your signature is stored once in the configuration.
IRONSTICK places it where you sign documents: in the print preview of the word processor and on the stamps of the annexes.
You can draw it with the mouse or on a touch screen, or upload a scan of your signature.

1. **Signatures.** Opens the signature card.
2. **Signature field.** Draw your signature with the mouse or your finger. *Clear signature field* starts again; *Upload image (JPG/PNG)* takes a scanned signature instead; *Delete* removes the stored signature; *Save* stores it. The green tick and the small preview show the signature currently stored.

## 5. Letterhead template

![The letterhead template card with the upload button](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/screens/A%20config%20template.png)

Your firm's letterhead under every pleading.

If your firm uses printed letterhead paper, upload it once as an A4 PDF.
IRONSTICK then puts your pleadings on this letterhead: the text starts below it.
In the word processor you switch the letterhead on or off for each document.

1. **Letterhead template.** Opens the card for the template.
2. **Upload template.** Choose the A4 PDF with your letterhead. As long as no template is stored, the card says so.

## 6. Bring in your clients

![The client list with the import window and the column assignment](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/screens/A%20client%20import.png)

Take your existing clients over from another program.

You do not have to type in your existing clients one by one.
Export them from your previous program or your address book as a CSV or vCard file and drag that file into the import window.
Even if you have never had a client management system of your own, data from your billing or invoicing software can often serve to import your client base directly — this saves a great deal of work.
New clients you create with *New client*.

1. **Import client.** Opens the import window. Drag a CSV or vCard (.vcf) file onto the drop area. An Excel file is first saved as CSV in Excel. For a CSV file, a second window lists each column with an example value and the field it goes into; the assignment is pre-filled and you can change every field. At least one column must be assigned to the name. With a single address, the *New client* form opens already filled in; with several, you tick in a list who is imported — clients that already exist are not ticked.
2. **New client.** Opens an empty client form for a client you enter by hand.

**And with that, you are ready to capture your cases in IRONSTICK.** Whether you enter all your existing files into IRONSTICK or simply start working with IRONSTICK on your new cases is up to you. In practice, a mix has proven to be the best way: capture by hand a few important existing cases that urgently need the support of IRONSTICK, and at the same time handle all new cases straight away in IRONSTICK.

Pricing

Modular licensing: pay only for what your law firm actually uses — and scale up or down at any time.

All prices are net (plus VAT). Licences are billed monthly; training modules are one-off on-site services. What the individual components do is described in the accompanying IRONSTICK sales document.

2. AI provider accounts

Recommended for running IRONSTICK: an API account with Anthropic (console.anthropic.com). It works like a prepaid credit account that you top up as needed, and it carries the core AI functions of IRONSTICK. IRONSTICK shows a visible warning in the app as soon as the credit account of a configured provider is used up. With the Bridges, these API costs drop substantially — all cost-intensive queries then run through the subscription instead of the API account.

Subscriptions with Anthropic, OpenAI, Qwen and Kimi are optional for running IRONSTICK — but become mandatory for running the respective Bridge. Subscription plans: Anthropic · OpenAI · Qwen · Kimi.

You are welcome to try the desktop apps beforehand — downloads: Claude Desktop · ChatGPT Desktop · Qwen Desktop · Kimi Desktop.

3. Training modules

Training is licensed separately, in three modules, all on site by a trainer — in one- and two-lawyer firms directly on the live case files, with the trainer under NDA; in larger firms first in groups as a lecture with worked examples.

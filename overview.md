# IRONSTICK at a Glance

> The law-firm software that knows your case files — local on your computer, with AI at your side.

Original page: https://www.ironstick.legal/overview.html

---

The law-firm software that knows your case files — local on your computer, with AI at your side.

IRONSTICK brings clients, cases, evidence, deadlines, e-mails and documents together in one case file — and gives you an AI that knows this case file inside out. You ask, the case file answers, with a reference to every document. You review and sign instead of searching and retyping. Everything is stored encrypted on your own hardware.

## 1. The Working Day

### The firm's day at a glance

![The firm's day at a glance](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/screens/1%20menu.png)

**The whole day on three pages.** Open IRONSTICK, and your day is already sorted.

- **Start.** All work areas and the status of your deadlines at a glance.
- **Agenda.** Everything that has come in across your cases, day by day — with document previews.
- **Heatmap.** Every day of the last twelve months as a square — the darker, the more has happened in your cases; deadlines and court dates are marked. Below it, the cases that have flared up: those with markedly increased activity over the last 14 days.

### Deadline monitor

![Deadline monitor](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/screens/11%20deadlines.png)

1. Reference numbers. The number of a document or an AI reminder is copied with one click — for example, to paste it into the chat.
2. Completing deadlines. Deadlines from documents and chronicle entries are ticked off and removed together; AI reminders leave the list via *Delete appointment*.
3. The AI also sets appointments and deadlines — directly from its conversation with you. They appear in the list with this symbol.

**Never miss a deadline.** A missed deadline is a liability case. IRONSTICK keeps all deadlines and court dates in one list and shows how much time remains.

- **All deadlines and court dates in one list** — with date, case and the days remaining until they fall due; overdue ones in red, those due soon in orange.
- **Deadlines from incoming documents.** The AI recognises deadlines in incoming letters and decisions and enters them.
- **Deadline bell.** Orange when a deadline falls due within the next ten days, red on the day itself — always visible in the menu bar.
- **Print the deadline list** or output it as a PDF.

### Automatic document capture

![Automatic document capture](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/screens/8%20capture%20documents%20proces.png)

**The inbox takes care of itself.** Scan, drag in, confirm: IRONSTICK recognises the text, assigns the document to the right case and enters the deadline.

- **Select pages.** Only the pages that belong in the case file are taken over.
- **Built-in text recognition.** For more than 35 European languages, nothing to install.
- **Suggestions instead of typing.** The AI suggests client, case, document type, language, date and deadline — you confirm.
- **Stored encrypted** and immediately full-text searchable.

Capturing a document thus takes barely more than 20 seconds — and the entire inbox is distributed to the case files in no time.

## 2. From Draft to Filing

### Word processing for pleadings

![Word processing for pleadings](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/screens/13%20texts%20wordprocesing%20preview.png)

1. Edit. The editable page with the full toolbar.
2. Preview. The document as individual A4 pages, exactly as they will be printed.
3. Print preview. Saves the document and opens the print preview with all annexes.
4. Save & close. Saves the document and returns to the list of texts.

**Pleadings in minutes instead of hours — without invented citations.** Your time is your revenue, and a wrong citation is the greatest risk when using AI. The draft is created in dialogue with the AI, directly from the case file — and every legal provision, every party and every piece of evidence is checked against the case file and the statute library before you see it.

- **From the case file.** Master data, chronicle, full texts, deadlines and annexes flow into the draft.
- **Edit and preview.** Change the text and see immediately how it will look printed on your letterhead.
- **Submission-ready template.** You review, finalise and sign.

### Print preview: pleading and annexes in one PDF

![Print preview: pleading and annexes in one PDF](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/screens/13%20texts%20wordprocesing%20printpreview.png)

1. Your document. The pages of the pleading — a left click on a page opens the window for placing your signature.
2. Annex title line. Entry number, annex number and title of each annex. Below it, its pages: a left click shows a page enlarged, a right click excludes it from the PDF; a second right click brings it back.
3. Certification stamp. Can be switched on and off for each annex; the stamp text follows the language of the case's country.
4. DOCX. The document as a Word file.
5. PDF download. The finished PDF from all active pages.

**The filing is ready.** Pleading and annexes stamped, paginated and signed in one PDF — instead of at the photocopier.

- **Place your signature.** Your stored signature is placed in the right spot with one click.
- **Annexes with certification stamp.** Each annex receives its stamp; individual pages can be excluded with one click.
- **Output as PDF or DOCX** — pleading and annexes in one file, paginated page by page.
- **For large proceedings too.** 3,000 pages stamped, paginated and merged into one PDF — in under 20 minutes.

### Dossier of the entire case

![Dossier of the entire case](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/screens/5%20case%20dossier%201.png)

**The complete case file in one document.** The dossier assistant compiles the entire case into a paginated dossier. First, you decide what goes in.

- **Select documents.** Every chronicle entry can be included or left out with one click.
- **Down to the individual page.** From each document you choose exactly the pages that belong in the dossier.
- **Look before you include.** Documents, photos, transcripts and chats open in the preview without changing the selection.

![Dossier of the entire case](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/screens/5%20case%20dossier%202.png)

**Cover page, structure and print view.** Next, you set the cover page and structure and check every page before the dossier is created.

- **Selectable cover pages.** You take the recipient from the case's institutions or enter it yourself.
- **In the language of the jurisdiction.** Cover page and indexes are created in the language of the case's country; if the country has several legal languages, you choose the language.
- **Structure as you wish.** Cover page, subject index, chronology, documents, media, transcripts, chats, index of persons and institutions — include, leave out and arrange by dragging.
- **Excluded pages visible at once.** The print view shows every page of the finished dossier; pages can still be left out or brought back there, and the subject index adjusts the page numbers.
- **Export as PDF or EDRM.** As a paginated PDF dossier or as an EDRM production package with Bates numbers.

## 3. Understanding the Case File

### Case assistant

![Case assistant](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/cutout_assistant.jpg)

**The case file instantly at your fingertips.** Before the hearing or the client meeting, you ask the case file instead of leafing through it — in natural language, by keyboard or by voice.

- “Summarise the current state of the proceedings.” — “Have we answered the opposing side's last letter?” — “What did the witness say about the site inspection?”
- **Every answer with evidence.** Every document cited is a clickable link to the entry in your case file.
- **Routine questions stay on your computer.** A local layer answers them without any data leaving the device.

### AI case assessment

![AI case assessment](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/screens/5%20case%20assesment%20detail.png)

1. The general case assessment takes the entire dossier into account, including the neighbouring cases.
2. The prospects of success take into account only the pleadings to and from the authority.

**Prospects of success you can explain to your client.** The AI case assessment reads the entire case file — your own pleadings as well as the opposing side's submissions — and sums up the state of the proceedings in one assessment.

- **Prospects of success on a scale** — with individual scores for the evidence, the main claim and further claims.
- **What speaks for you, and what against.** Strengths and weaknesses marked in colour, every statement with a link to the underlying document.
- **Concrete next steps** as a recommendation for how to proceed.
- **At the push of a button** — refreshed at any time as soon as new documents are in the case file.

### Network of persons and companies with OSINT research

![Network of persons and companies with OSINT research](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/screens/6%20entities%20universe.png)

**Uncover connections no one would find by hand.** Persons, companies, authorities and courts from all your cases form one shared network — as a list, as a 3D universe, as a 2D map and on the geographic map. A real gain, especially in commercial, insolvency and criminal law.

- **Connections with evidence.** Who appears in which proceedings, how the parties are connected to each other — and through what.
- **Three views.** The 3D universe to fly through, the 2D map of clusters and the geographic map with all parties and places.

**Your case file and the AI's OSINT research work together.** The AI combines what your case files know about a person or company with what public sources reveal. Every research turns into lasting knowledge about the parties — it grows with every case and is available to you in all proceedings.

- **OSINT research by the AI.** You name a person or company to the AI — it researches first in your own case files, then on the internet: career, social-media profiles, press, company holdings, commercial register.
- **Research becomes master data.** The AI creates new persons, companies and relationships, with their source, as proposals; only what you approve is adopted.
- **Decision analysis.** Every court decision in your case files can be analysed using a fixed grid — arguments, law, evidence, reasoning; before a pleading to that court, the AI reads the analyses as well.

## 4. Security and Operation

### Professional secrecy

![Professional secrecy](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/49_encryption.png)

**Professional secrecy stays in your firm.** No client data in third-party data centres: case file, evidence and documents are stored on your own hardware, [GDPR-compliant](https://www.ironstick.legal/gdpr.html).

- **AES-256 encrypted.** Database and documents are encrypted on disk.
- **Access with an authenticator code.** Your data appears only after you enter the six-digit code from your phone.
- **No client data sent to the vendor.** [External AI](https://www.ironstick.legal/ki.html) runs with your own key; calls are pseudonymised.
- **Works offline too.** Case file, search, documents and the local assistant run without internet.

### IRONSTICK SERVER

![IRONSTICK SERVER](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/screens/5%20case%20case.png)

**Working together — without the cloud.** The [IRONSTICK SERVER](https://www.ironstick.legal/download.html) links your workstations through a Synology NAS on your own network — without the cloud.

- **Share cases.** With all users or specifically with individual colleagues — every share can be withdrawn at any time.
- **Hand over a case.** A case moves completely to a colleague in a few clicks.
- **Encrypted on the NAS.** Shared cases are stored AES-256 encrypted on your firm's server.
- **Everyone keeps their case file.** The complete case file also remains on every workstation.
- **Shared statute library** and a shared network of persons and companies.

### Data backup

![Data backup](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/screens/18%20backup.png)

**Your entire firm backed up in one file.** One click on “Create backup”, and database and documents are saved in one encrypted backup file.

- **Save wherever you like:** locally, on an external SSD or on a USB stick.
- **With the IRONSTICK SERVER**, the backup is additionally stored encrypted on your firm's NAS.
- **Recent backups at a glance** — with date and size.
- **Restore** from a backup file in a few clicks.

**No IT effort — try it free for 30 days.** IRONSTICK runs on a standard Windows PC; updates are simply installed over the existing version, and all data is kept. Download the software from the [download page](https://www.ironstick.legal/download.html); the [Quick Start](https://www.ironstick.legal/manualquickstart.html) guides you in six steps from installation to your first clients.

## 5. System Requirements

### Client App and Bridge (optional)

|  | Minimum | Recommended |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Operating system | Windows 11, 64-bit | Windows 11, 64-bit, latest updates |
| Processor | Modern 4-core x64 (e.g. Intel Core i5 / AMD Ryzen 5) | 6–8 cores — text recognition processes several pages in parallel, and offline speech recognition benefits directly from every core |
| Memory | 8 GB RAM | 16 GB RAM — comfortable headroom for large PDFs, dossier creation and audio transcription side by side |
| Storage | SSD with 20 GB free | NVMe SSD with 100+ GB free — case archive, full-text search and PDF generation are disk-intensive; a fast SSD makes a noticeable difference |
| Display | 1920 × 1080 | 1920 × 1200 or more — the case screens make generous use of vertical space |
| Graphics | No dedicated graphics card needed — any onboard graphics will do |
| Connectivity | Internet only for external AI, statute updates, e-mail, web research, maps and address geocoding — case file, search, documents and the local assistant run fully offline; one free USB port for tablet sync |

### Storing data on an external drive (optional)

| Check | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Free space | At least 9 GB, or 1 GB more than the current data volume |
| Write / read | At least 80 MB/s / 150 MB/s — also under sustained load (not below 60 % of the initial value) |
| Many small files | At least 250 files per second |
| File system | NTFS or exFAT, no FAT32; not write-protected or locked |
| Reliability | Test data must be read back unchanged |
| Not permitted | Network drives and cloud-sync folders (OneDrive, Google Drive, Dropbox, iCloud, Box, pCloud, MEGA) |
| Recommendation | External SSD — ordinary USB sticks usually do not reach these values |

### IRONSTICK SERVER (optional)

|  | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Device | Any Synology NAS with DSM 7.0 or later — ARM or Intel; the package ships native binaries for ARMv7, ARM64 and x86-64 |
| Performance | Deliberately lean: verified on the **entry-level DS214se** with a single-core ARM CPU and 256 MB RAM; runs on every Synology model with DSM 7.0 or later, and current models offer considerably more headroom |
| Memory / CPU | No dedicated requirements beyond the NAS itself — the server does no analysis work; its background tasks are limited to relaying packages and a nightly clean-up |
| Disk space | A few hundred MB for the package and the statute library; shared case packages grow only with what you actually share |
| Network | Only the firm's LAN; one TCP port (47712) with token authentication. DSM, Web Station and ports 80/443 remain untouched |
| Administration | Any browser on the LAN; separate admin password; licence key entered once |

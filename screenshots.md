# IRONSTICK in action

> See IRONSTICK in action — screenshots of the legal case-management system: login, cases, evidence, AI chat, documents and more.

Original page: https://www.ironstick.legal/screenshots.html

---

A look inside the case file that works with you.

![Two-factor login — IRONSTICK screenshot](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/01_totp_login.png)

**Two-factor login**IRONSTICK unlocks only after a 6-digit code from your authenticator app. Background services start in the meantime, but no case data is shown until the code has been entered.

![The main menu — IRONSTICK screenshot](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/02_main_menu.png)

**The main menu**The day's essentials on one screen: overdue and upcoming deadlines, dormant cases, live security status, and direct access to clients, persons, institutions, documents, calendar, daily log, word processing, e-mail and global search.

![The always-on command bar — IRONSTICK screenshot](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/03_topbar.png)

**The always-on command bar**The top bar stays visible on every screen. A live drop-down shows what the connected AI is doing: the instructions it has read, any rule breaches detected and the number of documents processed. Alongside: the data monitor, e-mail, AI chat, IRONSTICK SERVER chat, the deadline indicator and a shutdown button.

![The agenda — IRONSTICK screenshot](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/04_agenda.png)

**The agenda**One timeline for past, present and future activity: deadlines and court dates, captured e-mails, chronicle entries and documents — from three days back to ten days ahead, so upcoming items stay in view.

![Activity heatmap — IRONSTICK screenshot](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/05_heatmap.png)

**Activity heatmap**A twelve-month heatmap of every working day — deadlines and court hearings marked in colour — with the currently most active cases listed below, so the busiest and most urgent matters stand out.

![The deadline monitor — IRONSTICK screenshot](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/06_deadlines.png)

**The deadline monitor**Every active deadline and court date in one paginated list — sorted by date, with client, case, hearing times and a clear countdown, so overdue and upcoming dates stand out immediately.

![The client list — IRONSTICK screenshot](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/07_clients.png)

**The client list**All clients in one list — name, city, phone, social media and e-mail, with a live case count per client and instant search by name, phone or e-mail. Selecting a client opens the file.

![A client's cases, grouped — IRONSTICK screenshot](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/08_case_groups.png)

**A client's cases, grouped**Every matter for one client, organised into groups you define — each case tagged by jurisdiction and type, with parties, status and work folders, plus direct access to the Party Universe.

![Shared notes on followed cases — IRONSTICK screenshot](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/09_notes.png)

**Shared notes on followed cases**Notes from colleagues following a case and from the AI — each entry stamped with author and time, so cross-matter insights stay with the file and everyone works from the same picture.

![Opening a new case — IRONSTICK screenshot](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/10_new_case.png)

**Opening a new case**A structured intake form: title, priority, status, opponent, co-parties with a built-in conflict-of-interest check, plus the legal block — case number, court-file date, country and legal area.

![Inside a case — the client tab — IRONSTICK screenshot](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/11_case_client.png)

**Inside a case — the client tab**One case, tabbed into client, case, institutions, entities, connections, map, documentation, timeline and AI — the client tab holding the full contact record.

![The case tab — IRONSTICK screenshot](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/12_case_case.png)

**The case tab**The heart of the file: title, case number, priority, parties, legal tags, court file, type and a full narrative description — with share, hand-over, print, edit and delete right at hand.

![Institutions on the case — IRONSTICK screenshot](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/13_case_institutions.png)

**Institutions on the case**Every court, prosecutor's office and authority involved in the case — numbered, classified and geolocated, each with direct phone, e-mail and web contact, so the institutional picture of a matter is in one place.

![Persons & entities on the case — IRONSTICK screenshot](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/14_case_entities.png)

**Persons & entities on the case**All parties to a matter — defendants, witnesses, experts, companies and associations — each with role, city, contact and a relationship analysis, paginated and searchable.

![The connection graph — IRONSTICK screenshot](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/15_case_connections.png)

**The connection graph**A network map of the whole case — 153 entities, 250 connections — persons, legal entities and institutions colour-coded, each edge a labelled relationship, all computed locally.

![The case map — IRONSTICK screenshot](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/16_case_map.png)

**The case map**Everyone and everything on the case, geocoded onto one map — persons, companies, institutions and events colour-coded, each pin opening its address and a jump into the file.

![The case documentation — IRONSTICK screenshot](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/17_case_documentation.png)

**The case documentation**The full chronicle of a matter — every letter, e-mail and filing, tagged inbound/outbound, cross-linked to its attachments and source entries, filterable and paginated.

![The case timeline — IRONSTICK screenshot](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/18_case_timeline.png)

**The case timeline**The same record as a chronological timeline — each entry with its parties, tags and linked attachments, so the story of a case reads straight down the page in order.

![Per-case AI instructions — IRONSTICK screenshot](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/19_case_ai_instructions.png)

**Per-case AI instructions**Upload up to ten TXT or MD files that the AI takes into account for this case only — private, case-scoped guidance that steers every assessment and draft.

![AI case assessment — IRONSTICK screenshot](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/20_case_ai_assessment.png)

**AI case assessment**On request, an AI-assisted analysis of the whole case with concrete recommended actions — built from the case-file entries and started by you.

![Preparing a bundle — IRONSTICK screenshot](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/21_prepare_dossier.png)

**Preparing a bundle**Choose exactly what goes into a case bundle — search and pick the entries you want, then continue to the bundle details and export a submission-ready bundle.

![Selecting document pages — IRONSTICK screenshot](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/22_select_pages.png)

**Selecting document pages**Down to the page: for each document you decide which pages go into the bundle, with a thumbnail preview of every page.

![Bundle print preview — IRONSTICK screenshot](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/23_print_preview.png)

**Bundle print preview**The whole bundle assembled — chronology and documents, numbered pages and blocks — previewed before you export it as a PDF or an EDRM ZIP.

![Exporting a document — IRONSTICK screenshot](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/24_export_document.png)

**Exporting a document**One dialog to finish: export the finished PDF straight to disk or to Google Drive.

![The persons & entities directory — IRONSTICK screenshot](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/25_persons_entities.png)

**The persons & entities directory**Every natural person and legal entity across all cases — searchable by name, phone or e-mail, with city, contact, a live case count and a relationship analysis.

![Adding a person or entity — IRONSTICK screenshot](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/26_new_person.png)

**Adding a person or entity**A structured record for a party: identity, address with optional geo coordinates, and the fields that let the system link and locate them.

![The Party Universe — IRONSTICK screenshot](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/27_universe_persons.png)

**The Party Universe**The whole web of a firm's parties on one canvas — persons, legal entities and institutions colour-coded, every edge a labelled relationship, computed locally.

![Person analysis — IRONSTICK screenshot](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/28_person_analysis.png)

**Person analysis**A full profile of one party: identity and residences, profession, education, employer and more — each field recorded with a reliability rating and the date it was established, plus web research on request.

![The entity detail card — IRONSTICK screenshot](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/29_entity_detail.png)

**The entity detail card**The full record of a party on one card — master data, address, contact, online presence and system fields — ready to export as a PDF.

![Sharing a contact by QR — IRONSTICK screenshot](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/30_entity_qr.png)

**Sharing a contact by QR**Any party's contact card as a QR code — scan it with a phone to save the contact in seconds.

![The institutions directory — IRONSTICK screenshot](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/31_institutions.png)

**The institutions directory**Every court, prosecutor's office and authority in one searchable list — classified and geolocated, each with direct phone, e-mail and web contact.

![Adding an institution — IRONSTICK screenshot](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/32_new_institution.png)

**Adding an institution**A structured record for a court or authority: classification, region, address with geo coordinates, and full contact details.

![The document capture inbox — IRONSTICK screenshot](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/33_capture_documents.png)

**The document capture inbox**Scanned, OCR-processed PDFs arrive in a monitored folder or can be dragged in directly, ready to be filed to cases.

![A scanned PDF ready to file — IRONSTICK screenshot](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/34_capture_documents_file.png)

**A scanned PDF ready to file**Each waiting document with its size and direct actions — view, capture into a case, or delete.

![Choosing pages to capture — IRONSTICK screenshot](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/35_capture_select_pages.png)

**Choosing pages to capture**Before filing, pick exactly which pages of a scan become evidence — each page shown as a thumbnail you can include or exclude.

![Filing a captured document — IRONSTICK screenshot](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/36_capture_document.png)

**Filing a captured document**Assign the document to a client and case and classify it — country, language, legal area, procedural stage, type, direction and date — in one structured step.

![Global search — IRONSTICK screenshot](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/37_global_search.png)

**Global search**One box across everything: titles, keywords, AI summaries, descriptions, parties, documents, full texts and legal briefs — all search terms must match, and results are ranked by relevance.

![The calendar — IRONSTICK screenshot](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/38_calendar.png)

**The calendar**Court appointments, witness examinations, meetings and phone calls in a two-month view — colour-coded, exportable as .ics, with the day's hearings listed below.

![The daily log — IRONSTICK screenshot](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/39_daily_log.png)

**The daily log**A cross-case journal, one entry per day — titles, linked cases and imported case-timeline events, paginated across the whole year.

![A daily log entry — IRONSTICK screenshot](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/40_daily_log_detail.png)

**A daily log entry**One day opened up: the linked cases and the full case timeline for that day — every event, cross-linked to its case.

![The Data Consistency Monitor — IRONSTICK screenshot](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/41_datamonitor.png)

**The Data Consistency Monitor**IRONSTICK monitors its own data — shown here: new and amended statutes detected for the jurisdictions of your cases, each with a source link, ready for you to accept or reject.

![The e-mail monitor — IRONSTICK screenshot](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/42_email_monitor.png)

**The e-mail monitor**The firm inbox, AI-triaged: each message is rated for urgency, with automatic case matching, ready to capture as evidence — with filters for important, all and hidden.

![The statute library — IRONSTICK screenshot](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/43_statutes.png)

**The statute library**A searchable library of statute texts — retrieved automatically or added by the AI, each with type, jurisdiction and source link; filter by your own or shared entries.

![Bridge set-up (local MCP server) — IRONSTICK screenshot](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/44_mcp_setup.png)

**Bridge set-up (local MCP server)**Let a local desktop AI — Claude, ChatGPT, Qwen or Kimi Desktop — reach IRONSTICK through a local MCP server, with separate switches for access and for letting it navigate the app. Optional, off by default.

![AI provider configuration — IRONSTICK screenshot](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/45_ai_config.png)

**AI provider configuration**Every provider you set up is used automatically in a fixed order, lowest cost first: Qwen, Kimi, ChatGPT, Claude. If one reaches its limit for the day, IRONSTICK switches to the next. Background tasks use the free tiers of Groq, OpenRouter and Mistral first, if you have set them up.

![The Party Universe — IRONSTICK screenshot](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/46_universe_flythrough.png)

**The Party Universe**The firm's entire network of parties — 576 parties, 40,000+ links, grouped into clusters — that you can navigate and explore, every node and connection computed locally.

![Navigating the Party Universe — IRONSTICK screenshot](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/47_universe_3d.png)

**Navigating the Party Universe**The same network in 3D: clusters you explore with keyboard navigation (W/A/S/D), approaching any node and following its links.

![The geographic map — IRONSTICK screenshot](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/48_geo_map.png)

**The geographic map**Every geocoded party and place on one map — clustered by proximity and zoom, so the geography of a firm's cases and contacts becomes visible.

![Encrypted at rest — AES-256 — IRONSTICK screenshot](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/49_encryption.png)

**Encrypted at rest — AES-256**Two-factor login and AES-256 encryption at rest: the database, the document vault and the configuration are encrypted on disk, so stored case files cannot be read from the disk of a lost or stolen device.

![Watch the AI work — live — IRONSTICK screenshot](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/50_two_windows.png)

**Watch the AI work — live**The command bar's live drop-down shows what the connected AI is doing — instructions read, rule breaches detected, documents processed — while you keep working in the main window.

![One system, every view — IRONSTICK screenshot](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/51_three_windows.png)

**One system, every view**Main menu, agenda and activity heatmap side by side — the whole practice in view, from today's deadlines to a year of workload and the most active cases.

![From client list to case file — IRONSTICK screenshot](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/52_clients_cases.png)

**From client list to case file**The client directory and a client's cases, grouped and tagged — from a name in the list straight to every matter, party and status in the file.

![Every angle on one case — IRONSTICK screenshot](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/53_case_tabs_collage.png)

**Every angle on one case**A single case seen through all its tabs — client, case, institutions, entities, connections, map, documentation and timeline — the complete file, one tab apart.

![Building a bundle, start to finish — IRONSTICK screenshot](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/54_dossier_flow.png)

**Building a bundle, start to finish**From selecting entries and pages, through the full print preview, to the finished export — the whole bundle workflow in one sequence, exported as a PDF or EDRM ZIP.

![Parties, records and the Party Universe — IRONSTICK screenshot](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/55_persons_universe.png)

**Parties, records and the Party Universe**From the persons-and-entities directory to a single record and into the Party Universe — every party, its data and its web of connections in one place.

![Capturing a document into a case — IRONSTICK screenshot](https://www.ironstick.legal/img/56_capture_flow.png)

**Capturing a document into a case**Drop a scanned PDF, pick the pages that become evidence, then assign it to a client and case and classify it — jurisdiction, legal area, type and direction — in one guided flow.

**The whole network, from every angle**From the parties directory to the geographic map, the 2D relationship graph and the 3D Party Universe — the entire firm's network seen from every angle, all computed locally.

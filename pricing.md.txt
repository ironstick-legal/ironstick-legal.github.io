# Pricing

> IRONSTICK pricing: modular monthly licences for the client app, the desktop-AI bridges and the office server, plus on-site training modules. All prices net.

Original page: https://www.ironstick.legal/pricing.html

---

Modular licensing: pay only for what your law firm actually uses — and scale up or down at any time.

All prices are **net** (plus VAT). Licences are billed monthly; training modules are one-off on-site services. Each component is described in the [Client App compendium](https://www.ironstick.legal/kompendium.html) and the [Bridge compendium](https://www.ironstick.legal/bridge.html).

- **Add-on modules & dependencies.** The Bridges and IRONSTICK SERVER are separately licensed add-on modules to the Client App. Each Bridge is unlocked in your Client App licence key, so no separate Bridge key is needed. The Central Scan App requires an active IRONSTICK SERVER licence (maximum 3 scan workstations per server).
- **Bring your own AI.** The built-in AI of the Client App runs on your API key with the provider — for the Client App alone, an API key is all you need. A Bridge is different: it works through the provider’s desktop app and your account there (e.g. Anthropic, OpenAI). You keep full control over your AI provider accounts and costs.
- **Flexible scale.** Add or cancel licences as your firm changes size, with no need to buy capacity in advance.

Try IRONSTICK free of charge for 30 days: the downloads and the trial licence are on the [download page](https://www.ironstick.legal/download.html).

## 2. AI provider accounts

**Recommended: an Anthropic API account**
([console.anthropic.com](https://console.anthropic.com)). The built-in AI of IRONSTICK runs on an API key from Anthropic, OpenAI, Qwen or Kimi; an Anthropic key is recommended because it also powers functions such as the AI web link search. An API account is a prepaid credit account that you top up as needed. IRONSTICK alerts you in the app as soon as a configured provider's credit is used up. **With a Bridge, the work you do with your desktop AI runs on your subscription instead of the API account**: a subscription is the provider's flat monthly fee, while API access is billed by the provider per use. Background tasks of the Client App run on the API keys or, if set up, the free tiers of Groq, OpenRouter and Mistral.

**A subscription with Anthropic, OpenAI, Qwen or Kimi is optional for IRONSTICK itself; the Client App alone needs only an API key; the corresponding Bridge works through that provider’s desktop app and your account there.** Subscription plans:
[Anthropic](https://claude.com/pricing) ·
[OpenAI](https://openai.com/chatgpt/pricing) ·
[Qwen](https://www.qwencloud.com/pricing/token-plan) ·
[Kimi](https://www.kimi.com/membership/pricing).

You can try the providers' desktop apps first:
[Claude Desktop](https://claude.ai/download) ·
[ChatGPT Desktop](https://openai.com/chatgpt/download) ·
[Qwen Desktop](https://chat.qwen.ai) ·
[Kimi Desktop](https://kimi.ai/).

## 3. Training modules

Training is offered separately in three modules, all delivered **on site** by a trainer. In firms with one or two lawyers, the trainer works directly on your live case files under a non-disclosure agreement; in larger firms, training starts with group sessions and worked examples.

| Module | Format | Western Europe (net) \* | Eastern Europe (net) \* |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **AI basic training** | 1 day on site | €2,200 | €1,500 |
| **AI expert training** | 1 day on site, extendable by the day | €2,500 per day | €1,800 per day |
| **OSINT special module** | on site, by the day | €2,500 per day | €1,800 per day |

\* Plus travel and accommodation expenses.

**Note on the AI expert training.** Additional days can be booked for advanced multi-model workflows, adversarial drafting and firm-specific AI instructions.

## Pleading drafts in minutes, not hours

Pleadings and letters are written in dialogue with the AI, directly from the case file. Before handover, a deterministic checker compares the draft with the live file: every evidence reference, every party, every legal citation.

The result is a submission-ready template, never a finished court document: you review, correct and sign, and the responsibility stays with you. Export as PDF or DOCX. Tested: 3000 pages of pleadings and exhibits, stamped and paginated, as one PDF in under 20 minutes.

## Your professional secrecy stays with you

The database lies encrypted on your own medium; the software vendor never sees client data. Consent before every external AI call, pseudonymization and an audit log are built in.

The Bridge deliberately transfers real data, under your own subscription with the provider. Your firm depends on no outside data centre: the licence is checked offline on your computer, and you can export everything you capture at any time.

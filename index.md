## Pleading drafts in minutes, not hours

Pleadings and letters are written in dialogue with the AI, directly from the case file. Before you receive the draft, an automated rule-based check compares every evidence reference, party and legal citation with the current case file.

The result is a submission-ready template that you review, finalise and sign. The fixed format is deliberate: from it, IRONSTICK assembles the finished filing itself — document and annexes in one PDF, page by page, with certification stamps, signature and annex page ranges that always match. Export as PDF or DOCX. In testing, 3,000 pages of pleadings and exhibits were stamped, paginated and combined into a single PDF in under 20 minutes.

## Your professional secrecy stays with you

The database and the captured case documents are stored AES-256-encrypted on your own hardware, access requires a six-digit authenticator code (TOTP), and IRONSTICK sends no client data to the vendor. External AI runs with your own API key; calls through the central AI router are pseudonymised and logged.

A Bridge connects your chosen AI provider directly to your case file, under your own subscription with that provider. Your firm stays independent of external data centres: the licence is validated offline on your computer, every case can be exported at any time as a PDF dossier or EDRM evidence ZIP, and a complete backup can be made with one click.

# Pricing

Original page: https://www.ironstick.legal/pricing.html

---

Modular licensing: pay only for what your law firm actually uses — and scale up or down at any time.

All prices are **net** (plus VAT). Licences are billed monthly; training modules are one-off on-site services. What the individual components do is described in the accompanying IRONSTICK sales document.

## 1. Licence prices

### Eastern Europe

| Component | Price (net) | Billing model |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **IRONSTICK Client** (base app) | €110 | per user / month |
| **Single Bridge** (Claude, ChatGPT, Qwen *or* Kimi) | €50 | per workstation / month |
| **All-Bridge Bundle** (all 4 Bridges: Claude, ChatGPT, Qwen and Kimi) | €90 | per workstation / month |
| **IRONSTICK Office Server** (incl. up to 5 clients) | €518 | per firm / server / month |
| **Additional client on the server** (from the 6th client) | €10 | per additional client / month |
| **Central Scan App** (max. 3 licences) | €25 | per scan workstation / month |

### Western Europe

| Component | Price (net) | Billing model |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **IRONSTICK Client** (base app) | €220 | per user / month |
| **Single Bridge** (Claude, ChatGPT, Qwen *or* Kimi) | €70 | per workstation / month |
| **All-Bridge Bundle** (all 4 Bridges: Claude, ChatGPT, Qwen and Kimi) | €130 | per workstation / month |
| **IRONSTICK Office Server** (incl. up to 5 clients) | €790 | per firm / server / month |
| **Additional client on the server** (from the 6th client) | €20 | per additional client / month |
| **Central Scan App** (max. 3 licences) | €40 | per scan workstation / month |

- **Add-on modules & dependencies.** The Bridges and the Office Server are separately licensed add-on modules to the base app. The Central Scan App requires an active Office Server licence (maximum 3 scan workstations per server).
- **Bring your own AI.** The desktop AI Bridges require an active user subscription or API access with the respective provider (e.g. Anthropic, OpenAI). You keep full control over your AI provider accounts and costs.
- **Scalability.** Add or cancel licences as your firm grows or shrinks. No long-term infrastructure over-provisioning required.

IRONSTICK can be tested free of charge for 30 days. The downloads are available on the [download page](https://www.ironstick.legal/download.html).

## 2. AI provider accounts

**Recommended for running IRONSTICK: an API account with Anthropic**
([console.anthropic.com](https://console.anthropic.com)). It works like a prepaid credit account that you top up as needed, and it carries the core AI functions of IRONSTICK. IRONSTICK shows a visible warning in the app as soon as the credit account of a configured provider is used up. **With the Bridges, these API costs drop substantially** — all cost-intensive queries then run through the subscription instead of the API account.

**Subscriptions with Anthropic, OpenAI, Qwen and Kimi are optional for running IRONSTICK — but become mandatory for running the respective Bridge.** Subscription plans:
[Anthropic](https://claude.com/pricing) ·
[OpenAI](https://openai.com/chatgpt/pricing) ·
[Qwen](https://www.qwencloud.com/pricing/token-plan) ·
[Kimi](https://www.kimi.com/membership/pricing).

You are welcome to try the desktop apps beforehand — downloads:
[Claude Desktop](https://claude.ai/download) ·
[ChatGPT Desktop](https://openai.com/chatgpt/download) ·
[Qwen Desktop](https://chat.qwen.ai) ·
[Kimi Desktop](https://kimi.ai/).

## 3. Training modules

Training is licensed separately, in three modules, all **on site** by a trainer — in one- and two-lawyer firms directly on the live case files, with the trainer under NDA; in larger firms first in groups as a lecture with worked examples.

| Module | Format | Eastern Europe (net) \* | Western Europe (net) \* |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **AI basic training** | 1 day on site | approx. €1,500 | approx. €2,200 |
| **AI expert training** | 1 day on site, extendable by the day | approx. €1,800 per day | approx. €2,500 per day |
| **OSINT special module** | on site, by the day | approx. €1,800 per day | approx. €2,500 per day |

\* Travel and accommodation are paid by the client.

**Note on the AI expert training.** The expert training can be extended by the day to optimise advanced multi-model workflows, adversarial drafting and firm-specific prompt engineering.

- Central Scan workstations scale with the volume of mail: up to about 10 lawyers one scan workstation is usually enough, larger firms use two or three (the maximum).
